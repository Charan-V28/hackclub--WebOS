# 💻 My WebOSMy WebOS is a interactive web-based operating system that runs directly in your browser. I built My WebOS step by step following the [Hack Club WebOS Jam](https://jams.hackclub.com/batch/guide.
---

## ✨ Features 
-Welcome Screen: A card that appears first and fades gently into the My WebOS desktop. -Desktop Environment: A custom background, a bar that shows status and icons for My WebOS apps.
-Live Clock: A clock that updates every second in the right of My WebOS.
-Interactive Windows: You can open, close and move My WebOS windows on your screen.
-Built-in Apps: Notes / Personal Profile: Several tabs that let you see About Me, My Skills and Stardance Goals in My WebOS. 

Projects App: Shows work and builds in My WebOS.🧮 
Calculator: A working calculator, inside My WebOS.

---## 🛠️ Built With
- HTML5. Provides the structure and layout of My WebOS.
- CSS3. Gives My WebOS a glass look sets up the grid adds animations and styles everything.
- JavaScript. Handles the clock window movement, drag and drop and keeps My WebOS state.
